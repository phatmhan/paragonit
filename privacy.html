1. Introduction

Paragon IT is committed to protecting the privacy of visitors to our website and clients who rely on our enterprise technology services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data in the course of our business.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that helps us communicate, deliver services, and improve our website experience. This may include:

  • Contact details such as name, email address, phone number, and company information.
  • Transactional or engagement-related information submitted through contact forms, proposals, or quote requests.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, device details, and usage patterns.
  • Third-party account information only when you choose to connect or authenticate through a supported service.

3. How We Use Information

We use personal data to:

  • Respond to inquiries, support requests, and service proposals.
  • Deliver our managed services, consulting, security, cloud and development engagements.
  • Maintain our website, analyze traffic, and improve our service offerings.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve site performance, remember preferences, and understand how visitors interact with our content. You can control cookies through your browser settings; however, disabling cookies may affect some site functionality.

5. Data Security

We apply industry-standard controls to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of personal data. Access to sensitive information is limited to authorized personnel, and we regularly review our security practices to address emerging risks.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share information with:

  • Trusted service providers who help us operate our website or deliver services.
  • Professional advisors or auditors when required for legal or regulatory compliance.
  • Third parties when necessary to protect our rights, prevent fraud, or comply with lawful requests.

7. International Transfers

Some information may be processed or stored outside your home jurisdiction. When we transfer data internationally, we rely on approved safeguards such as data transfer agreements or other lawful transfer mechanisms.

8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. You may also object to processing or request data portability. To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Please review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of our website or services after updates means you accept the revised policy.
