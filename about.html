Who we are

An engineering-first team built to run the unglamorous parts of enterprise technology exceptionally well.

Paragon IT was founded to close the gap between ambitious technology roadmaps and the discipline it takes to keep systems secure, available and fast. We work as an embedded technology partner — not a vendor that disappears after go-live.

Today our teams operate across software engineering, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity, data platforms and AI — supporting organizations that can't afford downtime, breaches or guesswork.

What we stand for

The principles that shape how we design, build and operate every engagement.

Engineering Excellence

Security by Design
