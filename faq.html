How can we help?

Grouped by the topics clients ask about most before, during and after an engagement.

General
What does Paragon IT actually do?

We're an enterprise technology partner. Our teams build and operate software, cloud infrastructure, cybersecurity programs, data platforms and AI systems for organizations that can't afford downtime, breaches or guesswork.

Which industries do you work with?

Primarily financial services, logistics, manufacturing and professional services — but our services are industry-agnostic. If you run critical infrastructure or handle sensitive data, we're likely a fit.

Do you only work with large enterprises?

Most of our clients are mid-size to large organizations, but we also take on growth-stage companies that need enterprise-grade security or infrastructure earlier than their headcount would suggest.

Engagements & Pricing
How does an engagement typically start?

With a scoping call, followed by a short technical assessment of your current environment. From there we propose a fixed-scope project, a retainer, or a phased roadmap depending on what you need.

Do you offer fixed-price or retainer models?

Both. Discrete projects (migrations, audits, builds) are typically fixed-price. Ongoing managed services, security operations and support are retainer-based with clear SLAs.

What's a typical project timeline?

It depends heavily on scope — a focused security audit can take 2-3 weeks, while a full cloud migration or platform build is usually measured in months. We give you a realistic timeline before any work starts, not after.

Security & Support
How do you handle data security and compliance?

Every engagement starts with a threat model. We follow least-privilege access, encrypt data in transit and at rest, and can operate within your existing compliance framework (ISO 27001, SOC 2, or sector-specific requirements).

Do you provide 24/7 support?

Yes, for clients on managed service and security operations retainers. Project-based engagements include support during business hours plus an agreed handover and warranty period.

Can you work alongside our in-house IT team?

That's the most common setup. We typically embed alongside existing teams rather than replace them — filling specialist gaps (security, cloud, data) or providing surge capacity for larger initiatives.

Still have questions?

If your question isn't covered here, reach out directly — a real engineer will answer, not a ticketing queue.
